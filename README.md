# Recovery Note

Recovery Note is a Minecraft Bedrock AddOn that lets players save and restore their entire player state using a special item.

Store your inventory, armor, XP, health, hunger, and active effects inside a Recovery Note, reset yourself back to a freshspawn-like state, then restore everything later exactly as it was.

Perfect for PvP kits, practice fights, dangerous exploration, minigames, and survival backups.

---

## Crafting Recipe

Craft a blank Recovery Note using:

- 1 Paper in the center  
- 8 Echo Shards surrounding it  

The recipe is intentionally expensive so notes feel valuable and cannot be spammed early game.

---

## How To Use

### Saving Your State

Right-click a blank Recovery Note to save:

- Inventory  
- Armor  
- Offhand  
- XP level and progress  
- Health  
- Hunger  
- Active potion effects  

After saving:
- The note becomes filled  
- Your inventory is cleared  
- Your player is reset to a freshspawn-like state  

---

### Restoring Your State

Right-click a filled Recovery Note to preview:
- Stored items  
- Health  
- Hunger  
- XP level  

Confirm the restore to recover your saved state instantly.

After restoring:
- The note is consumed permanently  
- The stored data is deleted  

---

## Features

### Full Player State Saving

Recovery Notes preserve nearly everything about your player, making them useful for:

- PvP loadouts  
- Dungeon runs  
- Adventure maps  
- Minigames  
- Hardcore backups  
- Practice fights
